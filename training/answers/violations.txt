Judgment on the negation case (dangerous):

The Mon social line is arguing *against* fear-selling: technicians are not busier
because spring is dangerous — they are less busy. The word list cannot see that
intent, so it flags a sentence that is doing the brand's job.

I would treat it as a review signal, not a hard ship-blocker. I would not remove
"dangerous" from the ban list — the list should stay blunt, or real fear-selling
slips through. Prefer rewrite: drop the banned word and keep the argument
("Our technicians are not busier in spring. They are less busy, which is the
point."). If rewrite is impossible, require an explicit operator override with
rationale rather than teaching the pipeline to auto-pass negation patterns
(those heuristics fail in both directions).

The other two hits are real: "Don't wait" is pressure; "cheapest" in the email is
a softer case but still on the rail and worth rewriting.
