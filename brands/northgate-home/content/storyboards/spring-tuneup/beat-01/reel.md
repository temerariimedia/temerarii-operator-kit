A driveway explainer. The technician walks the actual checklist while doing it — no
voice-of-god narration, no dramatic cuts. The persuasion is that we published the list
at all.

Rails for this beat: daylight only, real truck, real house. Orange accent appears once,
on the booking card at the end. No thermal imagery and no gauge shot framed to look
alarming. Calm conversational read; the acoustic bed never swells at the CTA.

## Slides
- Technician opens the panel. On-screen: "Seven checks. About 70 minutes."
- Refrigerant charge measured against spec. The reading is legible on screen.
- Outdoor coil cleaned — real dirt, real hose, no time-lapse theatrics.
- Capacitor tested under load. On-screen: "This is the part that usually goes."
- Temperature split across the evaporator. Number shown next to the manufacturer spec.
